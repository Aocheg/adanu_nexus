Design Practice

Graphic Design & Branding Questions

You receive all professional industry formats: scalable vector files (.AI, .SVG, .EPS), high-resolution web formats (.PNG with transparent background, .WebP), print-ready PDFs (CMYK 300 DPI with bleeds), and clickable Figma design links.
